## Production system

Joel and Ethan Coen transform a Chandler-like Los Angeles detective plot by giving it to the Dude, a protagonist who cannot control, interpret or even consistently remember the investigation. Mistaken identity, an allegedly kidnapped trophy wife, bowling friendships and competing explanations become an ensemble comedy built from precise production choices rather than loose improvisation.

The production system joins:

- Working Title, PolyGram and Gramercy independent-studio support
- contemporary Los Angeles locations and controlled stage work
- Roger Deakins’s colour 35 mm photography
- bright everyday reality interrupted by elaborate subjective fantasies
- Rick Heinrichs’s linked bowling, domestic, mansion, loft and modernist-house environments
- Mary Zophres’s sharply differentiated character costume system
- tightly scripted ensemble delivery led by Jeff Bridges and John Goodman
- Roderick Jaynes and Tricia Cooke’s information-withholding comic edit
- Allan Byer, Skip Lievsay and Michael Barry’s layered sound system
- Carter Burwell’s score and T-Bone Burnett’s source-music construction
- optical, mechanical and digital work for bowling-ball, flying-carpet and Busby Berkeley-style dream imagery

The result is neither a straightforward noir parody nor an unstructured slacker comedy. Genre confusion, character rhythm, Los Angeles architecture, bowling ritual, music and fantasy effects are designed as one coherent post-noir machine.
