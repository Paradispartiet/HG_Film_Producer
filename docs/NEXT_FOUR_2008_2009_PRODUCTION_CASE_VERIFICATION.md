## Production models

### The Class

The case treats the film as a workshop-built institutional fiction rather than an inspirational-teacher drama. François Bégaudeau's memoir is transformed through a school-year process with nonprofessional teenagers and parents, a fictionalized teacher performance, a classroom extended for technical access, three simultaneous cameras, Robin Campillo's multi-source editing and a sound field organized around interruption and contested authority.

Dedicated donors:

1. `scenario_toni_erdmann_2016`
2. `scenario_aftersun_2022`
3. `scenario_the_room_next_door_2024`

### WALL·E

The case treats the film as a Pixar constructed-world system joining silent-comedy behavior, ecological dystopia, color-scripted environments, photographed-camera simulation, Ben Burtt's robot language and Thomas Newman's score. Physical camera and lens research gives the virtual image focus limits, scale and imperfection rather than frictionless computer-animation movement.

Dedicated donors:

1. `scenario_2001_a_space_odyssey_1968`
2. `scenario_star_wars_1977`
3. `scenario_brazil_1985`

### A Prophet

The case models the film as a multilingual prison apprenticeship. A complete prison environment constructed at Gennevilliers, researched performances, Corsican and Arabic group boundaries, changing access to literacy and movement, Stéphane Fontaine's restricted observation, Juliette Welfling's six-year compression and institutional sound turn knowledge acquisition into the film's crime-production system.

Dedicated donors:

1. `scenario_clockers_1995`
2. `scenario_mesrine_killer_instinct_2008`
3. `scenario_out_of_the_past_1947`

### Alamar

The case treats the film as a small hybrid fiction-documentary production built around real family relationships and Banco Chinchorro. Pedro González-Rubio's multiple craft roles, a tiny mobile crew, a simple farewell structure, HDCAM observation, real fishing labor, environmental sound and openness to spontaneous animal encounters make ecology and family intimacy part of the same production method.

Dedicated donors:

1. `scenario_stranger_than_paradise_1984`
2. `scenario_paris_texas_1984`
3. `scenario_nebraska_2013`
