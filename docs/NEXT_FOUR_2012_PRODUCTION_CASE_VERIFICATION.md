## Production models

### In the House

The case treats writing as an active production and suspense mechanism. Claude's weekly essays, Germain's revisions and repeated access to Rapha's suburban family move continually among observation, narration, staged possibility and consequence. School and domestic geometry, voice-over, restrained performance, Laure Gardette's editorial handoffs and Philippe Rombi's score make authorship itself unstable.

Dedicated donors:

1. `scenario_the_game_1997`
2. `scenario_barton_fink_1991`
3. `scenario_being_john_malkovich_1999`

### Moonrise Kingdom

The case treats New Penzance as a complete hand-built 1965 world rather than a decorative Wes Anderson backdrop. Maps, records, scout procedure, uniforms, tents, canoes, family houses, Rhode Island coast, Super 16 texture, Benjamin Britten recordings and the approaching storm organize child escape, adult failure and reunion through one readable material system.

Dedicated donors:

1. `scenario_hugo_2011`
2. `scenario_midnight_in_paris_2011`
3. `scenario_the_truman_show_1998`

### Searching for Sugar Man

The case models the film as a transnational documentary investigation built from incomplete circulation history. South African fans, Detroit witnesses, records, photographs, newspapers, locations, animated reconstruction, Rodriguez's songs and recovered concerts turn archival gaps and death rumors into a mystery whose solution changes the meaning of the evidence.

Dedicated donors:

1. `scenario_all_the_beauty_and_the_bloodshed_2022`
2. `scenario_paris_is_burning_1990`
3. `scenario_american_splendor_2003`

### The Broken Circle Breakdown

The case treats bluegrass performance and nonlinear editing as the central family-production system. Van Groeningen and Carl Joos's theatre adaptation moves among courtship, parenthood, illness, loss and aftermath by emotional association. Veerle Baetens and Johan Heldenbergh's musical performance, Ruben Impens's intimate image, Nico Leunen's montage and Jan Deca's practical sound make the couple's ability to sing together a measure of their changing relation.

Dedicated donors:

1. `scenario_scenes_from_a_marriage_1974`
2. `scenario_dancer_in_the_dark_2000`
3. `scenario_the_sons_room_2001`
