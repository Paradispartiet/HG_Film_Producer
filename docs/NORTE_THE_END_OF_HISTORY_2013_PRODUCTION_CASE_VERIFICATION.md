## Production logic

The case is not treated as a generic slow film or placed inside a geographic Asia bucket. Its production logic joins:

- Lav Diaz and Rody Vera's redistribution of a Dostoevskian crime across murderer, prisoner and family
- Francis Fukuyama's end-of-history claim and the unresolved moral inheritance of Ferdinand Marcos
- Fabian's ideological freedom, Joaquin's wrongful imprisonment and Eliza's daily work as parallel consequence systems
- a 250-minute structure in which waiting, labour, travel and confinement receive material duration
- northern Philippine locations and changing colour from sunrise to sunset
- Larry Manda's anamorphic widescreen observation, static duration and restrained camera movement
- Perry Dizon's connected homes, streets, fields, prison and work environments
- Lav Diaz's own edit, which accumulates rather than accelerates consequence
- Corinne de San Jose's minimally manipulated environmental sound
- effects and conventional explanatory scoring remaining subordinate to social time
