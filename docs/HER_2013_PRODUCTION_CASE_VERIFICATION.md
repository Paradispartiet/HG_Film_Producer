## Production logic

The case is not treated as generic artificial-intelligence science fiction or as a romance with a technological premise added on top. Its production logic joins:

- Spike Jonze's first solo feature screenplay and its professional-letter-writing frame
- Joaquin Phoenix's embodied listening to a partner absent from the image
- Samantha Morton's live production performance and Scarlett Johansson's reconstructed postproduction performance
- dialogue revision continuing through recording and editing
- K.K. Barrett and Gene Serdena's tactile, car-light Los Angeles-Shanghai future
- Casey Storm's multi-decade, high-waisted, warm-colour wardrobe
- Hoyte van Hoytema's rejection of blue-and-steel futurism
- 35 mm texture, close facial observation and periodic wide city scale
- Eric Zumbrunnen and Jeff Buchanan's refusal of routine device cutaways
- Ren Klyce's discreet operating-system, interface and urban sound field
- Arcade Fire's score and songs as private composition, memory and emotional transition
- digital skyline and interface work remaining subordinate to human experience
