## Production-system interpretation

The seed correctly preserves horror as one of the playable genre pressures, but the source-backed Film Study case resolves through the existing **Constructed Worlds** family. The governing production system is not a generic creature attack. It is a child-restricted grief drama in which writing, performance, practical scale, illustration, animation, creature effects, image, editing, sound and music progressively make an unbearable emotional truth visible.

The production case joins:

- Siobhan Dowd's original idea, Patrick Ness's completed illustrated novel and Ness's own screenplay adaptation;
- sustained development among Ness, J.A. Bayona and producer Belén Atienza;
- the three embedded stories and demanded fourth truth as one grief structure;
- Lewis MacDougall's restricted child viewpoint across home, school, hospital and graveyard;
- Liam Neeson's reference performance and voice work for the Monster;
- a large practical Monster head giving MacDougall physical scale and eyeline;
- Eugenio Caballero's linked design of the yew, Conor's drawings, domestic spaces and destructive fantasy;
- Óscar Faura's ARRI Alexa XT and Hawk anamorphic photography;
- Glassworks and Headless animation that develops from watercolor and 2D imagery toward dimensional reality;
- MPC's 180-plus-shot yew-creature pipeline built from a lidar-scanned practical head, sliding wood plates, humanized eyes, keyframed acting, branches, transformations and destruction;
- Bernat Vilaplana and Jaume Martí's movement between routine, nightmare, tale and emotional release;
- Oriol Tarragó's award-winning sound design;
- Fernando Velázquez's score joining dread, intimacy, choir and acceptance.
