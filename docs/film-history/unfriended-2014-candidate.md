# Unfriended (2014) — candidate study

Chapter 30 · P2 · Not a canonical production case.

Unfriended uses the computer desktop as the frame of a fictional group conversation. Bekmambetov's account locates the premise in screen sharing, while Gabriadze situates the method in the lineage of found footage and Mike Figgis's Timecode. The classroom task is not to recreate the film's harmful events: it is to construct a consensual fictional conversation whose dramatic information is carried by screen activity. ([producer](https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/movies/la-et-mn-ca-unfriended-producer-20150412-story.html), [director](https://revistainteract.pt/41/the-haunted-interface-interview-with-levan-gabriadze/))

The ensemble was prepared through group video auditions and sustained rehearsals. Greaves describes a house arranged as separate character spaces connected by a shared audiovisual system. Olstead recalls coordinating her own framing with dialogue and using a laptop-mounted GoPro. This combines ensemble continuity with distributed framing; the approach should not be confused with Host's later remote-at-home production. ([camera](https://frontrowfeatures.com/features-category-of-interviews-and-spotlights/film-features/unfriended-ushers-in-screencast-movies-11548.html), [writer](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/96887/unfriended-qa-with-writer-nelson-greaves-and-producer-jason-blum/), [cast](https://www.shockya.com/news/2015/08/08/interview-renee-olstead-talks-unfriendeds-blu-ray-and-dvd-release-exclusive/), [fast](https://www.fastcompany.com/90605560/how-this-russian-directors-screenlife-films-went-from-gimmick-to-gold-in-hollywood))

The screen is also a post-production construction. Gabriadze describes editors Parker Laramie and Andrew Wesman manually animating desktop interactions. The distributor credits Adam Sidman as cinematographer and Heidi Koleto as production designer. The relevant craft distinction is between performers' recorded activity and constructed interface layers. Specific editing software, audio acquisition settings and final theatrical mixing workflow remain unclaimed here. ([director](https://revistainteract.pt/41/the-haunted-interface-interview-with-levan-gabriadze/), [credits](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/from-universal-pictures-home-entertainment-unfriended-300096176.html))

## Six-phase practice

- **Screen viewpoint and pitch:** Draft a fictional conversation that stays on one invented desktop and lasts two minutes.
- **Casting and ensemble rehearsal:** Rehearse a short fictional group call with agreed dramatic beats and a planned break.
- **Networked capture and blocking:** Draw a safe camera and eyeline plan for a three-person fictional video conversation.
- **Interface and cursor dramaturgy:** Animate a fictional message draft, pause and revision using original graphic assets.
- **Performance continuity and post:** Compare two short takes and design an editable desktop composition using the preferred performances.
- **Production economics and release:** Make a release ledger separating production-cost reports, festival premiere, later distribution and rights checks.
