# Film History — source-readiness review of the 12 existing Atlas definitions

Date: 2026-11-20. **Editorial evidence review only, not approval for playable use.**

This review covers the twelve exact title/alias + year matches among 101 book film references in Chapters 27–30. All existing Atlas definitions remain unapproved, and none of the book references gets a playable link. An institutional film listing confirms identity or descriptive context but cannot, by itself, validate technical production decisions, historical reconstructions, or game scoring.

## Three generic imported seed cases — film-specific design is missing

**Chapter 27: Festen / The Celebration (1998)** — scenario_the_celebration_1998; status imported_seed_needs_film_specific_design. Criterion confirms director Thomas Vinterberg, cinematographer Anthony Dod Mantle, Dogme 95 handheld filming and the film's collaborative craft. BFI confirms that the original Dogme 35mm condition was broken for the digital camcorder production. Technical **discrepancy to resolve**: Criterion's production notes name a Sony PC7, while BFI's 2022 early-digital interview names Sony PC3. Do not put either precise camera model into a correctness-scored case until primary production evidence resolves the difference. Replace the seed's generic drama presets with Dogme-specific lighting, handheld mobility, diegetic sound, family ensemble staging, explicit exceptions to the manifesto and carefully researched ethics around traumatic narrative material.

Sources: [Criterion film and crew credits](https://www.criterion.com/films/28059-the-celebration), [Criterion production notes](https://www.criterion.com/current/posts/7673-10-things-i-learned-the-celebration), [BFI early-digital history](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/features/attack-zeros-ones-early-years-digital-cinema-told-david-lynch-miranda-july-michael-mann-more).

**Chapter 30: Waltz with Bashir (2008)** — scenario_waltz_with_bashir_2008; status imported_seed_needs_film_specific_design. Director Ari Folman says the recorded interviews were edited into a video version, storyboards and about 2,300 illustrations before hybrid Flash, traditional and 3D animation. He explicitly says the finished film was **not rotoscoped** from recorded video. Replace generic animated-war-documentary choices with interview ethics, storyboarding, voice work, animation division of labour and distinct archival-versus-drawn imagery. Important: Folman's political interpretation of responsibility for the Sabra and Shatila massacre is **not** an independently established historical finding and must not become a grading rule; consult independent historians and investigations.

Sources: [Folman's official interview](https://www.sonyclassics.com/waltzwithbashir/aboutAri.html), [2008 Cannes press conference](https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/2008/press-conference-waltz-with-bashir/).

**Chapter 30: Tangerine (2015)** — scenario_tangerine_2015; status imported_seed_needs_film_specific_design. Academy Museum preserves an actual iPhone 5s and Moondog Labs anamorphic attachment from the production. Sean Baker explains FiLMiC Pro, 24fps recording and separate professional sound recording. He and Chris Bergoch describe months of research with people in the community, not an improvised story created solely by a phone. Replace seed's generic crime-comedy scoring with historically grounded phone/camera, stabilized movement, separate sound, casting collaboration, production consent and postproduction workflow.

Sources: [Academy Museum collection object](https://www.academymuseum.org/collection/sean-bakers-tangerine-iphone), [Sean Baker and Chris Bergoch interview transcript](https://www.sellingyourscreenplay.com/podcast-transcripts/sys-podcast-episode-110-sean-baker-chris-bergoch-talk-about-their-sundance-comedy-drama-tangerine-transcript/).
