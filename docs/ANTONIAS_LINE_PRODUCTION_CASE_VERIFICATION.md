## Production system

Marleen Gorris wrote the screenplay in 1988 and spent seven years securing finance. The resulting Netherlands-Belgium-United Kingdom co-production converts fifty postwar years into a female-line family chronicle framed by Antonia's final day.

The coordinated system combines:

- a cyclical screenplay about birth, life, death and continuation;
- Sarah's retrospective narration and large generational ellipses;
- a recurring farm, village, café, church and communal table;
- a large ensemble whose characters return across decades;
- limited rehearsal and script-led direction;
- Willeke van Ammelrooy and Jan Decleir's bodily ageing work;
- restrained makeup rather than heavy latex transformation;
- Jany Temime's costume continuity and Harry Ammerlaan's physical world;
- Willy Stassen's luminous colour 35mm photography in 2.35:1;
- episodic, cyclical editing by Wim Louwrier and Michiel Reichwein;
- stereo village, domestic and ritual sound;
- Ilona Sekacz's music;
- magical events presented as ordinary parts of an anarchic feminist fairy tale.
