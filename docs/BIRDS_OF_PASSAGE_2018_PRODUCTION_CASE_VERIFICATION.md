## Production system

Birds of Passage is materialized as a Wayuu-centered Colombian crime epic rather than a generic cartel or crime-thriller case. The production system joins the 1970s bonanza marimbera, clan law and mediation, female authority, ritual and oral-cultural structures, La Guajira geography, community-informed staging and a controlled photochemical image system.

The Film Study profile covers all 17 canonical production areas. Twelve are source-verified, four remain conservatively mapped where the inspected sources identify the department or formal function without documenting a complete method, and effects are marked not central.

## Source-backed decisions

- **History and screenplay:** official Cannes and Directors' Fortnight material identifies the 1970s marijuana boom, Wayuu family structure, María Camila Arias and Jacques Toulemonde's screenplay from an idea by Cristina Gallego, and the deliberate move away from a conventional male gangster viewpoint.
- **Community method:** Cristina Gallego describes working with Wayuu cultural specialist Wielder Guerra, roughly 30 percent Wayuu crew participation and community collaboration in rituals, spaces, environments and locations.
- **Image system:** David Gallego documents 3-perf 35 mm capture in 2.39:1 on an ARRICAM LT, spherical Ultra Prime lenses and zooms, three Kodak stocks, a mainly static observational camera and rarely used handheld movement.
- **Light and design:** the cinematography account documents strong-daylight stock choices, practical firelight, limited HMI support and archive-photo research with Angélica Perea for 1970s La Guajira buildings, objects, clothing and color.
- **Post and release:** Directors' Fortnight verifies Miguel Schverdfinger's editing, Carlos García and Claus Lynge's sound, Leonardo Heiblum's music and the film's 2018 world premiere as the section's opening film.
