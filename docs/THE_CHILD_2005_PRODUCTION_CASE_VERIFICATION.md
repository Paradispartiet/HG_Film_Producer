## Production system

The case is defined as `seraing_infant_sale_fatherhood_restitution`.

The coordinated system combines:

- an observed girl repeatedly circling Seraing with a pram as the originating image;
- a screenplay imagined around the missing young father;
- a love story and becoming-father story structured through sale, consequence and restitution;
- Bruno's present-tense economy of theft, cash, shelter, crime and exchange;
- Sonia's bodily care and refusal as the counterforce to Bruno's transactions;
- Les Films du Fleuve, Archipel 35, RTBF and Arte France Cinéma;
- Belgian, French and European public production support;
- Jérémie Renier and Déborah François inside extensively prepared physical action;
- rehearsal on actual locations before the technical system enters;
- Igor Gabriel's streets, apartments, shelters, river space, markets and criminal meeting points;
- Monic Parelle's contemporary material continuity;
- Alain Marcoen's colour 35 mm photography;
- Benoît Dervaux's handheld actor-camera relationship;
- Marie-Hélène Dozo's action-and-consequence edit;
- Jean-Pierre Duret and Benoît De Clerck's practical traffic, footsteps, doors, phones, breath and infant sound;
- the withholding of a conventional explanatory score;
- a Palme d'Or-winning moral transformation staged without psychological explanation.

The resolver remains `independent_storytelling` because the film's production problem joins post-documentary social evidence, embodied performance, real location, moral action, camera proximity, editing and practical sound.

The Child is resolved through a dedicated catalog helper before the Rosetta helper. Existing catalog profiles, global resolver ordering and donor pools remain unchanged.
