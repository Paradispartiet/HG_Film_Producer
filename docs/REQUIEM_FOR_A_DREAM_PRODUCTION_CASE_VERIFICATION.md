## Verified production system

- Darren Aronofsky and Hubert Selby Jr. adapt Selby's 1978 novel through four parallel addictions rather than a single drug narrative.
- Artisan Entertainment, Thousand Words and Protozoa mount the feature after Sundance-supported development, with Brooklyn and Coney Island location work.
- Matthew Libatique shoots 35 mm Panavision for 1.85:1 framing and creates a seasonal image progression from warm, softened summer to colder green winter.
- Practical fluorescent units, Kino Flos, softboxes, sodium-vapour, metal-halide and cool-white fixtures let lighting colour and temperature deteriorate with the characters.
- James Chinlund removes red from the production palette until Sara's red dress, making colour an explicit psychological event.
- Laura Jean Shannon, Carolyn Griffell, Judy Chin and Vincent Guastini coordinate clothing, wigs, bodily deterioration and prosthetic effects.
- Ellen Burstyn, Jared Leto, Jennifer Connelly and Marlon Wayans perform through physical preparation, extreme close framing, split screen and body-mounted cameras.
- Jay Rabinowitz builds repeated drug and pill rituals, time compression, split screens and an accelerating four-way climax into Aronofsky's hip-hop montage system.
- Brian Emrich works during shooting and picture editing, so pupils, pills, syringes, refrigerators, television and bodily impacts develop as image-and-sound units.
- Clint Mansell's electronic score and Kronos Quartet strings turn repetition into both pulse and tragic escalation.
- Jeremy Dawson and Dan Schrecker's Amoeba Proteus uses desktop compositing, broadcast noise, motion control and practical techniques for more than one hundred integrated effects.
- The film screened at Cannes in 2000 and Ellen Burstyn received an Academy Award nomination.
