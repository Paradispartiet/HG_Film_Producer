## Production Case purpose

This case closes the final Chapter 3 P1 gap by making **narrative time and represented memory** a distinct production problem.

The playable structure is not “invent the flashback.” It is:

1. organize crime, arrest, imprisonment, represented recollection and execution as one causal multi-tableau story;
2. make present confinement and remembered earlier life legible as different temporal layers;
3. preserve the scenic/theatrical construction of the memory episode rather than importing later standardized flashback grammar;
4. place Zecca's film inside Pathé's emerging organized fiction-production and genre system.

## Canonical safeguards

- Do **not** claim Ferdinand Zecca invented the universal cinematic flashback.
- Do **not** convert an early scenic represented-memory device into later standardized continuity grammar.
- Do **not** describe the photographed 1901 production as synchronized sound cinema.
- Do **not** isolate formal change from Pathé's production organization, longer footage and genre differentiation.
- Treat historical testimony about production cost, sales and speed as attributed archival evidence, not frictionless modern metadata.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Centre national du cinéma et de l'image animée (CNC)** — institutional evidence for Zecca, the 1901 film, longer footage, multiple tableaux and public success.
2. **Pathé** — company history for the 1901 launch of its film-production service and studio organization under Zecca.
3. **Fondation Jérôme Seydoux-Pathé** — institutional history for Zecca's production leadership and Pathé's differentiated genre production.
4. **GRIMH early-cinema archive** — attributed historical testimony concerning authorship, story, production, cost and sales.
5. **Historical Dictionary of French Cinema / Scarecrow Press–Bloomsbury** — scholarly secondary control for the early narrative and the two-plane prison/recollection staging.

The source set is intentionally plural: institutional company history does not carry the formal argument alone, and the scholarly staging description does not become a “first flashback” invention claim.
