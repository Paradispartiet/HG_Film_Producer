## Production system

Mike van Diem and producer Laurens Geels approached Ferdinand Bordewijk's material as dramatic raw material rather than as a text to reproduce reverently. The screenplay combines the 1938 novel Karakter with the earlier novella Dreverhaven en Katadreuffe, adds a murder-interrogation frame and replaces the novel's anticlimax with a fatal confrontation.

The coordinated system combines:

- a father-son conflict organised through debt, bankruptcy, law and social advancement;
- an investigation frame that turns most of the film into Katadreuffe's retrospective testimony;
- a roughly seven-million-guilder Netherlands-Belgium feature debut;
- First Floor Features and Almerica Film production structures;
- pre-scene rehearsal and story-led casting;
- Jan Decleir's monumental Dreverhaven and Fedja van Huet's breakthrough Katadreuffe;
- prewar Rotterdam reconstructed from surviving European urban fragments;
- Ghent, Antwerp, The Hague, Hamburg and Wroclaw locations;
- Wroclaw brick streets, neglected courtyards and interiors selected for material historical texture;
- production design by Rikke Jelier and Alfred Schaaf;
- Jany Temime's period costume system;
- Rogier Stoffers's colour 35 mm photography;
- a heightened, expressionist relation among architecture, weather, authority and movement;
- Jessica de Koning's murder-frame and life-history organisation;
- Marcel de Hoogd and Peter Warnier's credited production-sound and mixing system;
- Het Paleis van Boem's forceful score;
- a production principle that image, performance and editing must remain subordinate to dramatic clarity.

## Existing family

The resolver family is `european_time_identity` because Character constructs identity through remembered time, legal and paternal authority, withheld motives and a retrospective crime frame. The prewar city is not neutral background: it is reconstructed as the material pressure through which Katadreuffe becomes the person he believes he must be.

Character resolves through a film-specific catalog entry before the existing European time-and-identity profiles. It receives custom comparison feedback without entering the established donor pools for The Vanishing, Run Lola Run, The White Ribbon, Phoenix or Satantango.
