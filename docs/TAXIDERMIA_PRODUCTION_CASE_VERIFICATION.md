## Production system

The case is defined as `hungarian_three_generation_body_history_grotesque`.

The coordinated system combines:

- György Pálfi and Zsófia Ruttkay's transformation of two unrelated Lajos Parti Nagy stories into one three-generation genealogy;
- wartime servitude, state-socialist competitive consumption and post-socialist taxidermy;
- desire, appetite and self-preservation organized through sperm, spit and blood;
- Eurofilm Studio, Amour Fou, Memento Films, ARTE France Cinéma and La Cinéfacture;
- Sundance/NHK development recognition, Hungarian support and Eurimages-backed co-production;
- Hungary, Austria and France as production countries;
- Budapest and Vienna production in April 2005;
- Gergő Trócsányi's physical casting and Marc Bischoff's Austrian co-production casting;
- Adrien Asztalos and Géza Szöllősi's military, competitive-eating and taxidermy environments;
- Júlia Patkós's epoch-specific costume system;
- Hildegard Haide and the mask, makeup and prosthetic team;
- fabricated animals, enlarged bodies, wounds, fluids and anatomical objects;
- Gergely Pohárnok's colour 35 mm triptych image;
- Réka Lemhényi's genealogical transitions and bodily rhymes;
- Tamás Zányi's material field of eating, fluids, machinery and preparation;
- Amon Tobin's electronic continuity across three eras;
- the 2006 Cannes Un Certain Regard selection and Hungarian Film Week Grand Prize.

## Existing resolver

The resolver remains `independent_storytelling` because the film is an auteur-driven transnational production whose adaptation, historical triptych, body performance, designed environments, photography, editing, sound, music and practical effects form one inseparable storytelling system.

Taxidermia is resolved through a dedicated catalog helper before the existing special profiles and base catalog. Existing profile objects, donor pools, sorting, hash positions and feedback remain unchanged.

## Coverage decision

`source_verified`:

- historical context
- movement and tradition
- industry and production context
- reception and legacy
- screenplay
- directing
- performance
- production design
- costume and makeup
- cinematography
- camera format
- editing
- music
- effects and animation

`mapped`:

- lighting — three epoch-specific visual environments are evident, but the inspected sources do not document the complete lighting package or exposure strategy;
- sound design — Tamás Zányi and the material sound field are documented, but the complete production-to-final-mix workflow is unavailable.

`not_central`:

- documentary method — the film is a scripted, designed, performed and effects-built historical grotesque rather than documentary-informed production.
