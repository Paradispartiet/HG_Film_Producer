## Production model

The case treats the film as the first half of a French-Canadian-Italian two-film historical biography rather than a generic action scenario. Abdel Raouf Dafri and Jean-François Richet build the screenplay from Jacques Mesrine's prison memoir while treating that memoir as unstable self-presentation. Thomas Langmann and the international production partners sustain a nine-month shoot across France, Canada, Spain and the United States.

Vincent Cassel's performance links physical transformation, posture, voice and changing public identity. The production was photographed in reverse chronological order so the actor could move from the older figure toward the younger chronology. Robert Gantz's colour 35 mm photography, Emile Ghigo's period environments, Virginie Montel's costumes, Hervé Schneid's episodic edit, the César-winning sound team and music by Marco Beltrami and Marcus Trumpp provide one continuous craft system across the extended production.
