## Production logic

Gran Torino is modeled as a postindustrial Detroit community production rather than a generic redemption drama. The system joins:

- Nick Schenk and Dave Johannson's factory-rooted story and first screenplay;
- the move from Minnesota to Detroit after Michigan's 2008 film-incentive package;
- Malpaso, Village Roadshow and Double Nickel production;
- community searches in Detroit, Fresno and St. Paul for Hmong American performers;
- a largely first-time Hmong ensemble and Eastwood's early-rolling, limited-repetition method;
- Detroit-area homes, porches, garages, church, barber shop, construction sites and the preserved 1972 Ford;
- Tom Stern's restrained Panavision colour image;
- James J. Murakami's practical neighbourhood design;
- Joel Cox and Gary D. Roach's direct, reversal-led cutting;
- practical urban sound and the sparse Kyle Eastwood–Michael Stevens music system;
- Eastwood's conversion of the expected armed showdown into self-sacrifice.
