## Production system

The profile documents the coordinated relationship between:

- Kevin Smith's convenience-store experience and dialogue-led one-day screenplay
- View Askew's $27,575 independent production
- overnight access to the real Quick Stop and adjacent video store
- a cast and crew drawn largely from friends, family and local collaborators
- repeated counters, doors, shelves, shutters, roof and parking-lot geography
- David Klein's single-camera black-and-white 16mm photography
- fluorescent practical light used because a corrective lighting package was unaffordable
- Smith and Scott Mosier's compact episodic editing
- dense speech, store ambience, customer traffic and offscreen events
- Scott Angley's credited music and generational song texture
- Sundance premiere, Filmmaker Trophy Dramatic and Miramax acquisition
- New Directors/New Films and theatrical circulation
- more than $3 million gross, cult afterlife and 2019 National Film Registry selection
