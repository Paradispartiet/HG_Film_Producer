## Production system

The case is organised around one family's fragmented experience of the 26 December 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami rather than a multi-protagonist fictional disaster structure. Maria Belón's testimony, Sergio G. Sánchez's restricted screenplay and J.A. Bayona's direction keep information incomplete while the family is separated across water, damaged landscape, temporary care and hospital space.

The material production joins Thailand locations to Spanish water stages. Controlled currents, actor safety rigs and underwater systems are combined with one-third-scale resort models, full-scale debris, prosthetic injury, Super 35 photography, selected high-speed digital capture, layered sound and extensive compositing. The technical system is evaluated through how it preserves family viewpoint and historical responsibility, not through spectacle alone.
