- actual Thai resort, hospital and community locations
- Naomi Watts, Tom Holland, stunt performers and doubles working inside controlled currents
- practical debris and full-scale water interaction
- one-third-scale resort buildings and earlier small-scale water tests
- the large Alicante exterior tank
- Óscar Faura's mobile, restricted family viewpoints
- Eugenio Caballero's intact and destroyed environments
- Elena Ruiz and Bernat Vilaplana's separated-family editing
- practical impact, submerged, breathing and hospital sound
- Fernando Velázquez's score
- El Ranchito and partner teams' tracked digital extensions

The tsunami is therefore not encoded as a generic CGI event. It is a coordinated practical, miniature, location, performance, editorial and digital reconstruction built around bodily perception.
