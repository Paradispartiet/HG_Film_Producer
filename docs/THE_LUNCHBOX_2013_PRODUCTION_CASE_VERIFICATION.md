# The Lunchbox (2013) — Production Case verification

Verification date: **2026-07-29**

## Production-system decision

`scenario_the_lunchbox_2013` is materialized in the existing festival urban-intimacy resolver as:

`mumbai_epistolary_lunch_delivery_everyday_intimacy`

This is not a generic Indian, Mumbai or romantic-drama classification. The production system joins:

- Ritesh Batra's documentary research among Mumbai dabbawalas;
- one mistaken practical lunchbox as the transport system for an epistolary screenplay;
- two central characters who remain physically separated through most of the film;
- food preparation, office bureaucracy, trains and delivery labour as daily dramatic structure;
- real Mumbai streets, transport, interiors, dabbawalas and non-actor residents;
- restrained performances led by Irrfan Khan, Nimrat Kaur and Nawazuddin Siddiqui;
- parallel editing across preparation, delivery, reading and routine;
- detailed city sound, sparse music and an international postproduction process.
