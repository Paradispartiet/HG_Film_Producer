## Production model

The case treats the film as the distinct concluding movement of the Mesrine diptych rather than a duplicate of *Killer Instinct*. The first film constructs a criminal identity; the second begins once that identity is already public, contested and repeatedly staged through newspapers, television, political claims, disguises and police pressure.

Jean-François Richet describes the sequel as more frenetic and multi-axis than the first film, closer to a psychological pursuit thriller and filmed with more space around Vincent Cassel's established public figure. Abdel Raouf Dafri and Richet select the later-life episodes around prison authorship, public provocation, changing alliances and a known endpoint. Robert Gantz's colour 35 mm Scope image, Emile Ghigo's César-recognized production design, Virginie Montel's costumes, Hervé Schneid's episodic cutting, the shared sound team and music by Marco Beltrami and Marcus Trumpp complete the production system.
