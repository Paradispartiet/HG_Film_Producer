## Production system

The film was produced for roughly $3 million through Castle Rock and an American-Austrian production structure. The Vienna Film Financing Fund contributed approximately $500,000 after the production committed about $1.5 million of local expenditure.

The coordinated system combines:

- Linklater and Kim Krizan's encounter-based screenplay;
- substantial Hawke-Delpy workshop and rewriting work;
- weeks of intensive rehearsal;
- a two-person structure where each interaction motivates the next;
- a largely Austrian crew joined to Linklater's Austin core team;
- Austrian Moviecam equipment, local stock processing and real Vienna locations;
- bridges, cafés, record shops, churches, streets, transport and the Riesenrad as dramatic spaces;
- Lee Daniel's colour 35mm photography and sustained walking conversations;
- near-real-time romantic duration rather than conventional plot acceleration;
- Sandra Adair's first Avid feature and reaction-shot-centred dialogue editing;
- a restored 2.0 surround track carrying speech and changing city ambience;
- Fred Frith's credited score and recorded music embedded in the encounter;
- festival recognition, a nine-year-interval trilogy and National Film Registry preservation.
