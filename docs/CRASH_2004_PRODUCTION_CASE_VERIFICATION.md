## Production system

The case is defined as `los_angeles_interlocking_race_collision`.

The coordinated system combines:

- Paul Haggis's 1991 carjacking and later dream as the originating research problem;
- a spec screenplay written with Bobby Moresco from more than thirty initially conceived characters;
- one compressed Los Angeles day and night organized through parallel incidents, echoes and reversals;
- an independently assembled Bull's Eye, Bob Yari, DEJ, Blackfriars Bridge and Apollo production;
- a budget reduced from roughly $10 million to approximately $6.5 million;
- a mid-shoot financing collapse followed by emergency producer financing and additional DEJ support;
- CAA packaging and Don Cheadle's actor-producer commitment as the basis for assembling the cast;
- Sandra Bullock, Matt Dillon, Thandiwe Newton, Terrence Howard, Ludacris, Larenz Tate and a large reduced-fee ensemble;
- Los Angeles streets, municipal spaces, shops, homes, vehicles and neighborhoods treated as a social network;
- Haggis's own house and redressed interiors reused to extend the production beyond its resources;
- J. Michael Muro's colour 35mm CinemaScope photography of traffic, glass, headlights and close confrontation;
- Laurence Bennett's practical location-and-redress design across sharply separated class worlds;
- Hughes Winborne's parallel editing, delayed connections, recurring motifs and reflective pauses;
- practical crashes, fire, gunplay and snowfall supported by limited digital repair rather than effects-led spectacle;
- Mark Isham's restrained electronic-orchestral score and Bird York's recurring `In the Deep`;
- a Toronto premiere without a distributor, Lionsgate acquisition and unusually large commercial return;
- Academy Awards for Best Picture, Original Screenplay and Film Editing followed by a continuing dispute over both the win and the film's racial argument.
