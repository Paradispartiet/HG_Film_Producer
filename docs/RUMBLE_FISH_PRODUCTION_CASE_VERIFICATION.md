# Rumble Fish — Production Case verification

Verification date: **2026-07-24**

## Placement

`Rumble Fish` is treated as a single late New Hollywood / post-New-Hollywood auteur case, not as part of an arbitrary decade batch.

Its teaching system is the coordinated relationship between:

- S. E. Hinton adaptation and brother mythology
- Tulsa locations transformed into an expressionist wasteland
- Electronic Cinema monitoring and full video rehearsal
- stylized silent-film body language
- extreme wide-angle staging and psychologically changing camera movement
- high-gamma black-and-white photography and painted shadows
- time-lapse, in-camera and rear-projection effects
- short color interruptions inside the monochrome world
- Richard Beggs sound design and Stewart Copeland's percussive time system

## Runtime family

The profile resolves through `scenarioFilmStudyNewHollywoodBlockbusterBatch.ts` as a late extension of the New Hollywood family.
