## Production system

Call Me by Your Name is modeled as a literary-adaptation, desire, period-memory and Northern Italian location system rather than a generic romance template.

The source-backed production logic coordinates:

- James Ivory's adaptation of André Aciman's novel
- Luca Guadagnino's behavior- and location-led direction
- the summer-1983 Northern Italian setting around Crema
- Timothée Chalamet and Armie Hammer's performance preparation and growing on-set relationship
- Chalamet's Italian, piano and guitar preparation
- Samuel Deshors's material transformation of the villa property
- Giulia Piersanti's deliberately understated period wardrobe and local-family-photo research
- Sayombhu Mukdeeprom's Kodak 35 mm production
- a single KODAK VISION3 500T 5219 stock
- a deliberately simplified single-lens strategy
- Crema's natural light, colour and contrast as image design
- Walter Fasano's sensual, performance- and landscape-sensitive editing
- piano as character dialogue
- historically calibrated period-pop movement
- Sufjan Stevens's original songs and the production use of `Visions of Gideon`
