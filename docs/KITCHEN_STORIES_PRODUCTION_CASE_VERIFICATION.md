## Production system

Kitchen Stories is treated as a **postwar home-research friendship system**, not only as a generic Nordic deadpan comedy.

The playable model connects:

- the real Swedish Hemmens Forskningsinstitut and postwar kitchen rationalization;
- a Norwegian-Swedish coproduction with public-film and television backing;
- a screenplay organized by an observer protocol and a ban on interaction;
- Joachim Calmeyer and Tomas Norström's restrained, reciprocal performance;
- Billy Johansson's period kitchen, observation chair, diagrams and egg-shaped caravans;
- Karen Fabritius Gram and Eva Rygh's period costume and makeup system;
- Philip Øgaard's controlled 35 mm, 1.85:1 kitchen geography and winter exteriors;
- Pål Gengenbach's patient repetitions and delayed reaction cuts;
- Morten Solum and Petter Fladeby's sparse practical sound;
- Hans Mathisen's restrained musical punctuation;
- the gradual replacement of measurement, national suspicion and institutional distance with friendship.
