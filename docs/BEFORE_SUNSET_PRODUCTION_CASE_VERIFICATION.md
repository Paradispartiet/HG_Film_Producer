## Production system

Before Sunset is treated as a **Paris real-time reunion-dialogue system**, not as a conventional romantic sequel or an improvised walk-and-talk.

The playable model connects:

- nine years of real actor ageing after Before Sunrise;
- Richard Linklater, Julie Delpy and Ethan Hawke as collaborative screenwriters;
- an eighty-minute real-time structure governed by Jesse's departing flight;
- a compact fifteen-day Paris location production;
- Shakespeare and Company, café, streets, garden path, van, Seine boat and apartment as one emotional route;
- fully fixed dialogue whose apparent spontaneity depends on exact timing;
- Hawke and Delpy moving from public charm through disappointment, anger, confession and renewed attraction;
- Lee Daniel's colour 35 mm photography and Jim McConkey's unobtrusive Steadicam movement;
- late-afternoon natural light and difficult continuity across takes;
- two-camera photography inside live Paris traffic;
- Sandra Adair's reaction-shot, background and light matching;
- practical city, vehicle, river and apartment acoustics;
- Julie Delpy's guitar performance and Nina Simone's “Just in Time” as dramatic action;
- Berlinale competition, adapted-screenplay recognition and the trilogy's long afterlife.
