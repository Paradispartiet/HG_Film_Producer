Verified: **2026-08-06**

## Production system

Taxi is not treated as a generic character comedy. It is materialised as an Iranian dissident docufiction whose legal and material restrictions determine the film's complete form.

Jafar Panahi works under a filmmaking and travel ban, performs himself as a taxi driver and turns a yellow Tehran cab into a mobile studio. Dashboard-mounted cameras, devices handled by passengers, real traffic, episodic rides, ambiguous casting, absent approved credits and repeated debates about recorded evidence connect production method directly to censorship and distribution.

The comedy comes from social encounter, self-performance, interrupted driving and the gap between official rules and ordinary behaviour. The drama comes from punishment, inheritance, political imprisonment, image ownership and the danger attached to making or circulating what authorities call an undistributable image.
