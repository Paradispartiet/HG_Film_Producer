## Production system

The case is defined as `patagonian_nonprofessional_dog_road_fable`.

The coordinated system combines:

- an Argentine-Spanish coproduction led by Óscar Kramer and Guacamole Films;
- unemployment and economic displacement after Argentina's crisis;
- Patagonian towns, roads, service stations, workshops, homes and dog-show spaces;
- months of street-level casting for performers who resemble the people they play;
- Juan Villegas, Walter Donado and a largely nonprofessional ensemble;
- performers whose names and lived qualities overlap with their fictional roles;
- Gregorio, a reserved Dogo Argentino directed as an unpredictable partner rather than a sentimental prop;
- a written screenplay treated as a production guide rather than an inflexible authority;
- scenes adjusted around locations, behavior and events discovered during filming;
- computer editing during the shoot so captured material can reshape later scenes;
- Hugo Colace's 35mm observation of faces, roads, wind and Patagonian scale;
- Mohamed Rajid's understated journey rhythm;
- Nicolás Sorín's restrained score and a practical regional sound world;
- a documentary-fiction border that preserves real people and places inside an invented story.
