## Production system

Capernaum is materialized as a child-centred Lebanese social-realist production rather than a generic poverty drama. The production system joins direct research into children, paperless families, refugee displacement and informal survival with nonprofessional performers, real Beirut geography, chronological filming, highly responsive handheld camerawork, adaptive lighting and an unusually large editorial reconstruction.

The Film Study profile covers all 17 canonical production areas: 13 source-verified, 3 conservatively mapped and effects marked not central.

## Source-backed decisions

- **Research and screenplay:** Nadine Labaki describes researching difficult neighbourhoods, courts, children and parents across Lebanon and weaving observed situations, dialogue and anger into the screenplay rather than inventing a social world she had not lived.
- **Performance and directing:** Sony Pictures Classics and Labaki describe nonprofessionals whose lives closely parallel their roles; written scenes were allowed to change around spontaneous words, gestures and lived knowledge.
- **Production:** Christopher Aoun records a six-month, 90-day shoot with a roughly 50-person crew, mainly in Beirut and its suburbs, with the story filmed chronologically so inexperienced performers could accumulate experience inside their roles.
- **Camera and light:** Aoun documents an almost entirely handheld approach, child-height operation, ALEXA XT and ALEXA Mini bodies, broad lighting for improvisational movement and concealed CRLS/K-flector mirror trees in cramped locations.
- **Editing:** more than 500 hours of rushes produced a twelve-hour first version; Labaki describes editing as another rewrite, while Sony records the extended work with Konstantin Bock and Laure Gardette before and after Cannes.
- **Music:** Khaled Mouzanar abandoned a fuller melodic approach when it felt false to the performers' reality and retained music mainly for moments where the film deliberately shifts away from its dominant realist language.
- **Release:** Festival de Cannes records the 2018 Competition selection and Jury Prize.

## Donor architecture

The case stays inside the existing `independent_storytelling` resolver and uses the neutral `general` group. No new Lebanese, Arab or refugee-cinema global family is created from one film.

Its exact donor trio is:

1. `scenario_the_child_2005` — child care, illegal exchange, economic precarity and moral action expressed through bodily social realism.
2. `scenario_fish_tank_2009` — marginalized youth, close performance observation and a fiction shaped around a young nonprofessional lead.
3. `scenario_rosetta_1999` — embodied social realism, economic survival, real environments and camera proximity to a young protagonist under pressure.

Capernaum is deliberately excluded from the generic donor pool, so this specialty route cannot alter donor behavior for existing `general` films.
