## Historiographic safeguards

- Do **not** claim that *The Great Train Robbery* invented narrative film or editing.
- Do **not** retrofit later continuity cross-cutting onto Porter's 1903 robbers/posse organization.
- Treat the film as a strong convergence of production and formal practices already developing internationally.
- Treat the direct-address bandit gunshot as a flexible attraction/exhibition element rather than forcing it into a later fixed-ending rule.
- Keep later musical accompaniment separate from the photographed 1903 production.
- Distinguish real railway/location production from documentary method; the film remains staged fiction.
