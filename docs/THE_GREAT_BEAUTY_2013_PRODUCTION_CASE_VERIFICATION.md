## Production-system decision

`scenario_the_great_beauty_2013` is materialized in the existing European poetic-memory resolver as:

`contemporary_rome_baroque_writer_crisis_social_spectacle`

This is not a generic Rome, Italian or Fellini-reference classification. The production system joins:

- Paolo Sorrentino and Umberto Contarello's novel-like episodic screenplay;
- Toni Servillo's ageing writer as the route through Roman social worlds;
- palaces, terraces, churches, villas, streets and clubs as one urban theatre;
- fast three-camera coverage and mobile widescreen composition;
- lighting designed for complete 360-degree environments rather than isolated angles;
- montage that collides party density with reflective stillness;
- city sound, dance music, sacred music and silence;
- selective visible artifice inside an otherwise location-led production.
