## Production system

Elephant is treated as a **school-corridor repeated-time system**, not as a conventional explanatory school-shooting drama.

The playable model connects:

- the immediate post-Columbine media context;
- HBO Films and Dany Wolf's Portland production framework;
- abandonment of a conventional causal screenplay;
- local high-school students playing characters developed from their own lives;
- a real closed school used as branching corridor geography;
- Gus Van Sant and Harris Savides's extended 35 mm tracking movements;
- 1.37:1 Academy framing and fast 500 ASA stock;
- practical hallway lighting and limited window augmentation;
- repeated encounters edited from different characters and temporal positions;
- mobile MS stereo, practical school ambience and Leslie Shatz's expressionistic sound design;
- musique concrète, Beethoven and Arvo Pärt entering through environment and subjective attention;
- refusal to reduce catastrophe to one motive, diagnosis or warning-sign chain.
