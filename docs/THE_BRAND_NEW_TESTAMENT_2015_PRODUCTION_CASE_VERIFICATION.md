## Production system

The case is not treated as generic fantasy comedy. It is modelled as a Belgian-French-Luxembourg constructed-world production in which a child's fairytale rebellion, Brussels locations, religious frontality, theatrical fabrication and selective effects form one system.

Jaco Van Dormael and Thomas Gunzig wrote the screenplay together over six months. The chain of six new apostles is deliberately episodic: each chapter has its own emotional problem, colour, visual treatment and music while Ea's journey and the released death dates hold the parts together.

The visual system combines:

- frontal, symmetrical framing derived from churches and portrait photography
- Brussels locations and a deliberately cramped, ugly divine apartment
- Sylvie Olivé's real-surreal production design
- Sony F65 capture, Sony F55 mobile work and Leica Summilux spherical lenses
- moving theatrical lights, pocket torches and character-specific colour
- miniature cardboard Brussels, cotton clouds and visibly fabricated stage ideas
- a life-size operated gorilla and selective digital effects
- associative editing and direct-address monologues
- tactile sound intended to suggest touch, smell and material presence
- apostle-specific Baroque and popular source music plus An Pierlé's piano score
