## Production system

Film-specific system:

`barents_state_church_property_expropriation_landscape_tragedy`

This is not a generic Russian crime drama or a location-based festival classification. The production system joins:

- the Marvin Heemeyer property dispute, Heinrich von Kleist's *Michael Kohlhaas*, the Book of Job and Thomas Hobbes;
- Russian public and private financing through Non-Stop Production, the Ministry of Culture, the Cinema Fund and RuArts;
- a municipal attempt to seize one inherited coastal property;
- a multigenerational house built specifically for the film rather than inherited as a ready location;
- a garage, greenhouse, boats, road, utility poles and pier constructed to bind the home to the landscape;
- one planned demolition shot that determined both construction and camera position;
- a fabricated and anchored whale skeleton functioning as material fact and political-biblical image;
- a replacement church whose lower façade was built physically before CGI completed the upper walls and roof;
- a 55-day production moving between Teriberka, Moscow interiors and winter return photography;
- roughly six months of composition and lighting preparation by Andrey Zvyagintsev and Mikhail Krichman;
- soft northern half-light, fog and overcast conditions instead of direct sun;
- colour 35 mm Scope photography processed through CineLab;
- restrained performance joining domestic routine, official speech, alcohol and betrayal;
- patient legal and domestic duration shaped by Anna Mass;
- Andrey Dergachev's sea, wind, engines, rooms, courtroom language and demolition in 7.1;
- Philip Glass material from *Akhnaten* as circular institutional pressure.

The result makes state, church, law and family visible as one production system. Buildings, roads, landscape and sound do not merely illustrate corruption: they determine how power can enter, value and finally erase a private life.

## Existing resolver

The existing resolver remains:

`european_time_identity`

Leviathan continues the documented Zvyagintsev–Krichman concern with authority, family identity, withheld information, landscape and controlled time already represented by *The Return*. Its distinct branch adds property law, municipal procedure, church-state rhetoric, large-scale practical construction and physical-digital destruction.

The new profile is resolved before the earlier film-specific branches. The original core profile object, generic donor pool, sorting and hash positions remain unchanged.
