## Verified production system

The Production Case is modeled as a transatlantic auteur, outsider-ensemble and music-led location system rather than as a conventional murder mystery.

- Bono's rooftop impression of the real Downtown Los Angeles building developed with Nicholas Klein into the story; Klein wrote the screenplay.
- Road Movies, Kintop Pictures and Icon Productions mounted the German-American production, with Wim Wenders directing.
- The actual hotel, roof, corridors, lobby, rooms and surrounding Downtown streets provide the production's social and architectural core.
- Jeremy Davies, Milla Jovovich, Mel Gibson and the wider resident ensemble organize competing forms of innocence, self-invention, investigation and communal performance.
- Phedon Papamichael photographed the film on 35 mm in approximately 2.35:1 CinemaScope, allowing the decayed architecture and ensemble spacing to dominate the frame.
- Robbie Freed and Arabella Serrell transformed existing rooms through accumulated books, paintings, Beatles relics, scavenged furniture and resident-specific objects.
- Nancy Steiner's costumes and the makeup, hair and prosthetic departments differentiate the residents, Eloise's literary self-presentation and Skinner's rigid investigative body.
- Tatiana S. Riegel's credited editing joins Tom Tom's voice-over to freeze frames, monochrome passages, jump cuts and investigative crosscutting.
- Lee Orloff, Elmo Weber and the sound team layer hotel rooms, corridors, Downtown streets, testimony and investigation around the subjective narration.
- Bono, Brian Eno, Daniel Lanois and Jon Hassell create a song-and-ambient system that connects the residents' inner worlds to the larger music culture.
- SpaceCam, the two roof falls and changes in image state support Tom Tom's unstable perspective without turning the film into effects spectacle.
- The film opened the 50th Berlinale, received the Jury Grand Prix and later received a 5K restoration and 4K DCP through the Wim Wenders Foundation.
