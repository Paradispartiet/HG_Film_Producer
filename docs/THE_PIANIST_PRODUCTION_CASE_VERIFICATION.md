## Verified production system

The Production Case is modeled as a survivor-memoir, restricted-viewpoint and reconstructed-wartime-city system rather than as a generic prestige war drama.

- Ronald Harwood adapts Władysław Szpilman's memoir, keeping occupation and genocide legible through one civilian's changing access to family, streets, work, hiding places, food and music.
- R. P. Productions, Heritage Films, Studio Babelsberg and Runteam mount a French-Polish-German-British co-production directed by Roman Polanski.
- Warsaw's surviving streets, Babelsberg's backlot, Jüterbog barracks and other German and Polish locations are combined into the ghetto, occupation city and ruined capital.
- Adrien Brody's progressive physical depletion, observation and silence provide continuity while social institutions and urban geography collapse around Szpilman.
- Allan Starski and the art departments transform architecture through barriers, signs, interiors, debris and destruction rather than treating historical background as decorative spectacle.
- Anna Sheppard, Didier Lavergne, Waldemar Pokromski and the wardrobe, hair and makeup teams track restriction, forced identification, displacement and bodily deterioration.
- Paweł Edelman photographs the reconstruction on 35 mm in 1.85:1, moving from crowded public space to isolated rooms and exposed ruins without adopting a mixed-media newsreel surface.
- Hervé de Luze compresses years through abrupt separations, repeated relocations and withheld transitions; the editorial process remains mapped rather than department-level source-verified.
- Jean-Marie Blondel, Dean Humphreys and Gérard Hardy organise dialogue, crowds, bombing, gunfire, footsteps, rooms, distant battle and silence as a progressively emptied urban sound field.
- Wojciech Kilar's score and Janusz Olejniczak's Chopin performances connect profession, memory, imagined touch, danger and the final recognition of Szpilman's identity.
- Optical work, practical destruction, smoke, fire, wounds and extensions support the reconstructed city, while the complete effects breakdown remains mapped.
- The film won the 2002 Palme d'Or, three Academy Awards, BAFTAs for film and direction, seven Césars and the European Film Award for cinematography.
