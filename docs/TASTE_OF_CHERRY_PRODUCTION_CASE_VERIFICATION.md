## Production system

Abbas Kiarostami reduces a road film to repeated drives, individual faces, offscreen conversation and the excavated hills outside Tehran. Mr. Badii's unexplained intention to end his life is approached through encounters rather than backstory.

The coordinated system combines:

- an Iran-France production written, directed, produced and edited by Kiarostami;
- the preparatory sketch film `Project`;
- Homayoun Ershadi maintaining Badii's emotional condition across roughly two months;
- nonprofessional passengers filmed separately;
- Kiarostami occupying the opposite seat as unseen interviewer and provocateur;
- a camera fixed to the side window, hood or passenger position of the moving car;
- reflections and partial facial obstruction accepted as part of the image;
- Homayoun Payvar's colour 35 mm photography;
- repeated routes through Tehran's quarries, earthworks, roads and industrial margins;
- an edit that constructs conversations from separately recorded performances;
- Jahangir Mirshekari's location sound and Mohammad Reza Delpak's monaural mix;
- engines, machinery, wind, birds, thunder and offscreen voices extending space;
- a final shift from the 35 mm narrative to lower-resolution video of the crew;
- an ending that replaces conventional closure with the visible production process.
