## Result

## Production system

The profile documents the coordinated relationship between:

- László Krasznahorkai's circular novel and its six-forward, six-back tango structure
- Béla Tarr and Krasznahorkai's non-literal translation from prose into cinematic duration
- a Hungarian-German-Swiss post-socialist co-production
- two years of preproduction and 120 shooting days spread across two years
- late-autumn and early-spring dawn-and-dusk production conditions
- nearly 100,000 metres of 35mm raw stock and approximately 150 scenes
- Gábor Medvigy's black-and-white 1.66 photography
- static observation and complex choreographed tracks
- real mining and industrial locations assembled into one fictional settlement
- professional actors, amateurs, collaborators and composer Mihály Víg in the cast
- actor improvisation inside tightly planned camera and spatial structures
- Ágnes Hranitzky's on-set rhythm control and editing inside the take
- György Kovács's sustained environmental sound world
- Mihály Víg's precomposed music used during shooting
- practical wind, rain and constructed environmental conditions
- the 1994 Berlinale breakthrough and the 2019 4K restoration from the original negative
