## Production logic

The case is not treated as a generic family drama or a generic Woody Allen comedy. Its production logic joins:

- Jasmine's reduced San Francisco present and reconstructed New York past
- associative flashbacks that revise rather than merely explain the present
- Cate Blanchett's socially polished but physically unstable central performance
- Sally Hawkins's contrasting sister performance and shared-history preparation
- Santo Loquasto's class-coded New York and San Francisco environments
- Suzy Benzinger's repeated, borrowed and deteriorating luxury wardrobe
- Javier Aguirresarobe's widescreen actor and social-space observation
- Alisa Lepselter's memory-triggered temporal transitions
- public dialogue, involuntary self-address, city ambience and recurring jazz
- effects remaining non-central to the film's instability
