## Production system

Charlie Kaufman's screenplay turns unstable authorship and identity into a literal portal system. Spike Jonze's feature debut keeps the impossible premise physically ordinary rather than presenting it as disconnected spectacle.

The production system joins:

- a roughly ten-million-dollar Propaganda Films and Single Cell Pictures production
- a script long regarded as commercially unmakeable
- close line-by-line development between Kaufman and Jonze
- a rewritten third act built around emotional consequence
- the low-ceiling seven-and-a-half floor as workplace satire and physical compression
- a portal tunnel, embodied first-person viewpoint and precise ejection rule
- John Malkovich performing himself, his public image and several character states
- K.K. Barrett's office, portal, domestic and performance architecture
- Lance Acord's restrained colour 35 mm photography and 1.85 framing
- Eric Zumbrunnen's portal transitions, identity handoffs, mock-media inserts and time shifts
- camera-built marionette performances, body doubles and compositing
- Carter Burwell's warm, melancholic and circular score
- instructional film, pseudo-documentary and celebrity self-performance used as fabricated evidence

The result makes architecture, authorship, desire and performed identity inseparable. Production design, performance, point of view, editing, sound and effects all obey the same material logic.
