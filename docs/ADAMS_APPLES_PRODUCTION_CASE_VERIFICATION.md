## Production system

The case is defined as `danish_theological_grotesque_rehabilitation_fable`.

The coordinated system combines:

- Anders Thomas Jensen's original modern Book of Job screenplay;
- a neo-Nazi sent to community service at a rural vicarage;
- a priest whose radical optimism functions as denial and survival method;
- a contained ensemble of damaged residents, doctor and visitors;
- M&M Productions, DR, August Film and Danish-German participation;
- cast-specific writing for Ulrich Thomsen and Mads Mikkelsen;
- repeated script readings and on-set rehearsal before controlled improvisation;
- moderated performance rather than broad comic signalling;
- Mia Stensgaard and Anders Engelbrecht's church, vicarage, kitchen and apple-tree world;
- Jane Whittaker's ideological, clerical and practical costume contrasts;
- Thomas Foldberg and Louise Hauberg Nielsen's injury and medical makeup;
- Sebastian Blenkov's colour 35 mm CinemaScope ensemble framing;
- Anders Villadsen's editing, including the removal of irony that broke emotional truth;
- Nino Jacobsen and Morten Degnbol's restrained dialogue, ambience, storms, birds and violence;
- Jeppe Kaas's score and the Bee Gees refrain `How Deep Is Your Love`;
- Robert-winning visual effects for the escalating attacks on the tree and Ivan's body;
- a final reconciliation that remains both miracle, joke and moral argument.
