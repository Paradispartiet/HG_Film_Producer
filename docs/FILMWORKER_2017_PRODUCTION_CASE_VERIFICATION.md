## Production thesis

Filmworker is not primarily a documentary about Stanley Kubrick's finished films. Its production argument is that auteur cinema depends on practical workers whose contributions can disappear behind the credited director. Tony Zierra makes Leon Vitali's labor visible through long-form testimony, collaborator interviews, film excerpts, behind-the-scenes material, stills and production artifacts.

The production system therefore coordinates:

- Zierra's original Kubrick-documentary research changing direction after meeting Vitali;
- Vitali's transition from actor in *Barry Lyndon* to Kubrick's all-purpose collaborator;
- casting and actor coaching, including Danny Lloyd and work with later Kubrick performers;
- location, set and production problem-solving;
- print checking, color timing, sound and release-quality control;
- marketing, translation, preservation and restoration work;
- interviews with collaborators who corroborate, complicate and contextualize Vitali's account;
- archive and film excerpts used as evidence rather than decorative cinephile quotation;
- Tony Zierra's own cinematography and editing of the contemporary documentary layer;
- a small True Studio Media production centered on Zierra and producer Elizabeth Yoffe.
