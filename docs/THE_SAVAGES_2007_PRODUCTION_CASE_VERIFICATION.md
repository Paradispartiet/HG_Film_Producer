## Production system

The case connects:

- Tamara Jenkins's family experience with dementia and nursing homes, transformed into a fictional two-sibling screenplay;
- a project that began as a five-page scene in 1997 and survived a financing break over Jenkins's insistence on Laura Linney and Philip Seymour Hoffman;
- This Is That, Ad Hominem, Cooper's Town, Lone Star and Fox Searchlight production and financing;
- Linney and Hoffman as competing adult siblings, with Philip Bosco's embodied decline preventing the father from becoming an abstract plot device;
- a thirty-day location production across Sun City, New York City and Buffalo;
- real hospitals and nursing-home environments and Jane Ann Stewart's contrast between retirement fantasy, winter city and practical care space;
- Mott Hupfel's performance-responsive handheld colour 35 mm image and 1.66:1 presentation;
- Brian A. Kates's dry performance rhythm and procedural travel-and-care structure;
- practical institutional sound and Stephen Trask's restrained musical system;
- the Sundance premiere, AFI recognition and Academy nominations for Laura Linney and Jenkins's original screenplay.
