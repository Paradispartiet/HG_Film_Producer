## Production thesis

The Square is not treated as generic art-world satire. Its production system is a **contemporary-institution behavioural laboratory**: a real public trust experiment becomes a fictional museum artwork; prestige architecture and public space become social-test environments; actors repeat long situations until behaviour replaces declared psychology; Fredrik Wenzel's ARRI ALEXA, spherical 1.85:1, shallow-focus and contrast-rich image deliberately departs from Force Majeure; Josefin Åsberg's design keeps the museum plausible while allowing the world to edge toward surrealism; and Östlund with Jacob Secher Schulsinger edits a very large take archive around awkward thresholds, group responsibility and duration rather than conventional comic coverage.

Terry Notary's gala sequence is a concentrated example: a dialogue-free physical intervention develops in relation to hundreds of assembled guests, allowing uncertainty and bystander behaviour to become production material rather than merely scripted reaction.
